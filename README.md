### 🏭 AI-Powered Coal Mine Governance & Compliance Monitoring

An AI-assisted system designed to help monitor safety and compliance
conditions in coal mines.

**Key Ideas:**

- 🎥 Camera-based monitoring
- 🦺 Safety equipment detection
- 🚧 Restricted-zone monitoring
- 👤 Unauthorized-entry detection
- 🔔 Violation alerts
- 📋 Violation reports
- 📸 Evidence-based verification
- 👮 Officer-assisted corrective action

> The system is designed to **assist responsible officers**, not replace
> government or mine officials.

**Problem Statement:** SIH26024  
**Team:** Go-Gitters
